## Known limitation shipped with this deploy

Months with no collected data at all (2025-11 … 2026-05) are absent from the
timeline, the month drill-down **and the coverage grid** — their `pending`
coverage entries exist in `data/dashboard.json` but are not rendered, so the
coverage summary reads "10/18" (displayed window) while the contract-window
figure is 10/39. Reported as issue #7 with a refresh-checklist gate in
`docs/deployment.md`; deliberately not fixed in the test-only PR #5.
